# Campaign qualification follow-up

Before automated appointment booking is enabled, extend Services and Service Products with reusable qualification criteria.

Planned fields:

- Qualification questions and acceptable answers
- Required versus optional criteria
- Disqualifying answers
- Qualification score or outcome
- Campaign decision-node mapping
- Audit history for who changed the criteria

Campaigns should reference a versioned snapshot of these criteria so an active campaign is not changed unexpectedly when a service or product is edited.
